Privacy Policy for TorSoft-ERP

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

TorSoft-ERP is a Windows desktop application that allows users to back up their local database and application files directly to their personal Google Drive account.

2. Google User Data Accessed

When you connect your Google account, TorSoft-ERP requests access to Google Drive solely to create, upload, and manage backup files generated by the software on your own Google Drive storage.

3. How We Use, Store, and Share Data

  • Data Usage: Your Google Drive access is used strictly to upload and restore your TorSoft-ERP backup files at your direction.
  • Data Storage: TorSoft-ERP runs locally on your Windows PC. Your backup files are transferred directly from your computer to your personal Google Drive. We do not collect, transmit, or store your files or Google credentials on any external developer servers.
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with any third parties, advertisers, or AI models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

TorSoft-ERP's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You can revoke TorSoft-ERP's access to your Google Drive at any time through your Google Account Permissions page. You may also delete your backup files directly from your Google Drive at any time.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: torsoft.erp@gmail.com
